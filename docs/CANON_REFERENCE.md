# Canon reference

Four layers, never blended. Anything the deck does not support and no other
source establishes is marked TBD rather than invented.

## 1. Pitch-deck canon (DECK_CONFIRMED)

- **I.B.S. — Intergalactic Broadcasting Services**, run by **Gorlëënyah** with
  her AI sidekick **Visqueen**. CHOKE HOLE is its gladiator reality bloodbath.
- **The Pinkstar**, Gorlëënyah's abandoned bio-mechanical ship. **Qashir**, the
  ancient consciousness inside it, manifests as a giant eyeball and is
  capitalism incarnate.
- **Squelsh**, I.B.S.' top sponsor: an energy drink that "calms you down".
- **IT Factor** is what Qashir actually consumes. Squelsh is a byproduct; Qashir
  is building himself a body.
- **Jassy**, billionaire SHE-E-O of H.E.R., founder of the rival **CHOKE H.E.R.**
- **RAID**, six-legged bug-boy created by Squelsh experimentation, accidental
  child of Gorlëënyah, secretly organising against I.B.S.
- CHOKE HOLE began in **2018** in a New Orleans warehouse: trash-built ring,
  drunk stunts, permits forged for a fashion show.

## 4. Game-original

Everything mechanical. Each move, taunt and prop carries a `provenance` field in
the data:

- `REAL_VERIFIED` — from footage or performer confirmation. **Currently none.**
- `GAME_ADAPTATION` — built on something the deck establishes. Jassy's giant
  phone (the deck and her reference photographs show a brick phone; scaling it
  to an absurd size is ours). The Squelsh can and forged-permit props.
- `GAME_ORIGINAL` — invented for the game. All strikes, throws, dives, signature
  and finisher names.

Move names come from Bible s14 and are explicitly placeholders. The
union-themed naming the Bible suggests for RAID is flagged there as "to be
approved", so it is not on a shipped move.

## What the game never claims

- No performer photograph is used anywhere. All art is procedural geometry.
- The 2018 warehouse is a stylised composite. No street address or floor plan is
  implied, because the research does not establish one.
- No real venue name ships without its source tier displayed beside it.
