# Move research — RAID

**Performer:** Pat (Patrick) Wolf.
**Character:** an anti-gentrification mutant insect. Described in press as a
"neon-colored bug".

## Research method and its limits

Gathered by web search in September 2026. **Direct page fetches are blocked by
this environment's network proxy**, so every entry comes from search-engine
extracts of the listed articles rather than from reading them in full. Entries
are `REAL_VERIFIED` only where the extract quoted or directly described the
action.

No match video was available to this environment. **Nothing here is derived from
watching footage.**

### R2 — The Silly String prop
- **Event:** CHOKE HOLE x Superchief Gallery NY
- **Year:** 2019
- **Opponent:** Jassy
- **Description:** RAID "'came' all over Jassy" with a Silly String-shooting
  phallic prop, to a big crowd reaction. Jassy then rips it away from him.
- **Category:** PROP SPOT / COMEDY
- **Source:** VICE
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED

### R3 — Character origin (press)
- **Description:** RAID is described in press as "a Covergirl Makeup test
  subject mutated into a giant pro wrestling bug", and as a bug "whose home was
  fracked by RuPaul".
- **Note:** This differs from the CHOKE HOLE 10.3 pitch deck, which gives his
  origin as Squelsh experimentation and makes him Gorlëënyah's accidental child.
  Both are recorded; **the deck is the higher authority for the game's canon**
  (Bible s2), and the press version is kept here as the live-show origin.
- **Category:** CANON
- **Source:** Gothamist; WUSSY Mag
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED (as published description)

### R4 — He builds the props
- **Description:** CHOKE HOLE's props are mostly carved car Styrofoam, a
  technique RAID, Jocelyn Change and others learned working at Mardi Gras float
  companies. The first two shows used stages made from old tires covered in
  plywood, and a billboard for local personal injury attorney Morris Bart.
- **Category:** PRODUCTION / PROP DESIGN
- **Source:** WUSSY Mag
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED
- **Game use:** props should read as hand-carved foam, not as clean CG objects.

### R5 — The gentrification rivalry
- **Description:** RAID is the unwanted tenant/squatter against Jassy's real
  estate agent. He is the face; the crowd is with him.
- **Category:** RIVALRY / STORYLINE
- **Source:** Gothamist; VICE
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED

### R6 — Troupe fundamentals
- **Description:** Back bumps, dodging, running the ropes, clotheslines — the
  vocabulary CHOKE HOLE teaches in its own classes.
- **Category:** STRIKES / ROPE MOVES / BUMPS
- **Source:** Verite News (2025); WWNO
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED (troupe-wide)
