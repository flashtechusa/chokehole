# Move research — JASSY

**Performer:** Jassy Gordon. Co-founder of CHOKE HOLE (with Hugo Gyrl / Ellery
Neon / Gorlëënyah, and Visqueen).
**Character:** an insidious real estate mogul — described in press as a "jumbo
cellphone-flaunting real estate mogul" and as "Kris Jenner-esque".

## Research method and its limits

Gathered by web search in September 2026. **Direct page fetches are blocked by
this environment's network proxy**, so every entry below comes from search-engine
extracts of the listed articles rather than from reading the articles in full.
Entries are marked `REAL_VERIFIED` only where the extract quoted or directly
described the action.

No match video was available to this environment. **Nothing here is derived from
watching footage.** When the team can supply footage or corrections, the
`REAL_VERIFIED` set should grow considerably and the `GAME_ORIGINAL` set should
shrink.

### J1 — Entrance: the black briefcase, to "Work Bitch"
- **Event:** CHOKE HOLE x Superchief Gallery NY, World Pride Edition
- **Year:** 2019 (event listing: 27 June 2019, presented by POWERHOUSE ARTS)
- **Opponent:** RAID

  lip-syncing to "Work Bitch". Costume described as pink and orange gem tones.
- **Category:** ENTRANCE
- **Source:** VICE, *These Glorious Photos Show Off the Subversive World Where
  Drag Meets Wrestling*
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED

### J2 — The jumbo cellphone
- **Event:** Choke Hole, Queens NY
- **Year:** 2019 era
- **Description:** Press describes Jassy in the ring as a "jumbo

- **Category:** PROP (signature prop)
- **Source:** Gothamist, *Inside Choke Hole, A Spectacular Drag Queen Wrestling

- **Event:** Choke Hole, Queens NY
- **Year:** 2019 era
- **Opponent:** RAID
- **Description:** "The audience booed as Jassy strangled Raid with a giant

- **Category:** PROP SPOT / SUBMISSION
- **Source:** Gothamist

### J4 — Disarming RAID's Silly String prop and the reversal spot
- **Event:** CHOKE HOLE x Superchief Gallery NY
- **Year:** 2019
- **Opponent:** RAID
- **Description:** RAID shoots Silly String at Jassy from a phallic prop; Jassy
  "ripped that figurative phallus out of Raid's hands, knocked her opponent to
  the ground, and mimed shoving the can up Raid's ass." The crowd turns on her
  at this point.
- **Category:** PROP SPOT / REVERSAL / HEEL TURN BEAT
- **Source:** VICE; Gothamist
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED

### J5 — The gentrification storyline
- **Description:** The Jassy vs RAID match is built as a real estate agent
  against an unwanted tenant/squatter, dramatising gentrification. The crowd
  alignment (RAID face, Jassy heel) follows from it.
- **Category:** RIVALRY / STORYLINE
- **Source:** Gothamist; VICE
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED

### J6 — Troupe fundamentals
- **Description:** CHOKE HOLE's own wrestling classes teach back bumps, dodging,
  running the ropes (darting from one side of the ring to the other and bouncing
  off), and clotheslines (an outstretched forearm to the chest prompting a fall).
  These are the troupe's actual working vocabulary.
- **Category:** STRIKES / ROPE MOVES / BUMPS
- **Source:** Verite News, *Queer wrestling troupe Choke Hole show fans the
  ropes* (2025); WWNO
- **Status:** REAL_VERIFIED (troupe-wide, not Jassy-specific)

## Not documented

**No named signature or finisher for Jassy appears in any source reachable from
here.** Searches for a signature/finisher tied to the giant phone, the briefcase
or ARMAGEDDON returned nothing. Her signature and finisher in the game are
therefore `GAME_ORIGINAL` and are flagged for replacement.

Also undocumented: her rope/corner repertoire, her taunt vocabulary, and whether
the briefcase is used as a weapon as well as an entrance prop.
